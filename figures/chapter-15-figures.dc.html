Scope Questions and Scale Questions

“
The prompt
"I have a contractors marketplace app used in hundreds of cities worldwideScale. Contractors can search for clients and bid on jobs. I want to add a vendor catalog feature. Parts suppliers can upload product catalogs and keep them up to dateScope. How would you design this?"
Scope
(functional questions)
8
1Who uploads the catalogs, the suppliers themselves or an admin?
2What formats can they upload? CSV, PDF, their own API feed?
3What is in a catalog? Names, descriptions, prices, photos?
4Does it need real-time availability, or is approximate stock fine?
5Do vendors push updates, or do we pull from their inventory?
6Can contractors order in the app, or is the catalog reference only?
7Is search across all catalogs, or per vendor?
8Are prices public, or negotiated when a contractor makes contact?
Scale
(non-functional questions)
5
1How many vendors do we support at launch?
2How many SKUs does a vendor carry?
3How often does a vendor send an update?
4Do vendors specify the region they supply?
5How often is each catalog or item updated?
The hints in this prompt
"Keep them up to date"This is not a one-time upload.
"Hundreds of cities worldwide"The scale is international.
