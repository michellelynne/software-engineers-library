Seven Forms of Respect

Type
Definition
Actions
Procedure
Following established formal processes, instructions, and rules
When requests are fulfilled exactly as asked, and when clear operational guidelines are set and maintained
Punctuality
Honoring people's time.
Arriving on time or early to meetings, ending them on schedule, and providing advanced notice if you will not meet that criteria
Information
Providing access to data, files, and updates
Copying colleagues on emails, inviting them to meetings, and proactively offering background context without being asked
Candor
Offering clear, actionable feedback
Being honest, engaging in constructive, unemotional debates, and asking challenging questions as an equal peer
Consideration
Anticipating and adapting to other people's needs, feelings, and comfort
Interacting with interpersonal sensitivity and proactively making adjustments to help minimize burdens on others
Acknowledgment
Expressing explicit appreciation and recognition
Publicly or privately praising people for their contributions, validating their hard work, and ensuring they feel seen and heard
Attention
Actively listening and being fully present
Removing distractions and focusing entirely on the person speaking without interrupting
