FIGURE 11.4

What the Breaker in Your Wall Is Actually Doing

Normal load
9 amps
The contacts touch, so current runs from the supply straight through to the load.
Overload
31 amps on a 20 amp line
Too much current heats a strip of metal inside the breaker until it bends and snaps the contacts apart. The circuit goes dead in a fraction of a second, on purpose, and the rest of the house stays lit.
Reset
One try
Someone walks to the panel and pushes the handle back. It half opens and tries again. If the fault is still there, it trips again immediately. If it is gone, the circuit is live again.
