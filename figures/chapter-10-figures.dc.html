FIGURE 10.1

Three Lists, Three Cadences: Who Hears What, and How Often

Palest and smallest, smallest message
Size and shade both track the size of the message. The management list is the small pale center, because it needs the least: a short read on impact, rarely. Each step outward gets darker as it gains detail and frequency, out to the sign-up list, which asked for everything.
Outermost, largest
Sign-up list
Who
Anyone who asked to be there. First to try a new app, plus developers who want to integrate your tool into something else.
What they get
Development updates. Work in progress, rough edges, what is landing next, and an open door for feedback.
How often
Frequently
Middle
Stakeholders list
Who
Everyone who will be impacted, whether or not they signed up for anything.
What they get
Targeted release updates that acclimate them to change coming, always paired with how to use the new tools.
How often
A few times, at milestones
Innermost, smallest
Management list
Who
The people who approved the work and answer for the budget behind it.
What they get
Impact and adoption. What the tool changed, who is using it, and how far along the migration is.
How often
Rarely
Same rule, all three
Let anyone move outward. Someone on the stakeholders list who wants every detail should be able to join the sign-up list without asking permission.
Figure 10.1  Detail and frequency both grow as the circle widens. Match the message to the ring rather than sending one update to everybody, which either drowns the innermost list or starves the outermost one.
FIGURE 10.2

The OAuth Client Credentials Flow, All Inside the SDK

What the vendor writes
A client ID and secret, once, at initialization.
What the vendor never handles
The token, its expiry, and every refresh.
Figure 10.2  The machine-to-machine version of OAuth: long-lived credentials are exchanged for a short-lived token, the token does the work, and the SDK replaces it before it expires. Because the caller never touches the token, revoking one integration does not disturb the others.
