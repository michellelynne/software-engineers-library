Goal Type: Autonomy

The goal of this section is to help you identify your highest-priority growth goals and how to reach them. Instead of just answering me, I want you to take the time and review all of the options. Some of them might surprise you and sneak their way to the top. Or maybe there are some you want to wait on.

This worksheet is standardized so you can use it for all of the types: respect, influence, autonomy, sense of accomplishment, and financial. For each one, you will find a breakdown brought to you by an expert in their field. For any that resonate with you, check out their book in further reading. Once you complete a worksheet for each goal type, the Growth Mode worksheet will help you put it all together.
