Networking Preparation

Networking is a skill that can be improved with practice, not a personality type. Use this worksheet to understand what is preventing you from networking and make a plan to get you in the room and make the most of it. Networking anxiety can come from improvisation, and this will help you know what to say.

02

Networking Goal

"Meet five people" is not a goal, it is a commit count. Pick something that helps your career and that a stranger could reasonably help with. A job referral is what most people want and the hardest thing to get from someone who has known you for ten minutes. Having a goal also means you can leave when you are exhausted. It also gives you a topic to get to so you’re not stuck in dead end small talk. It should be something accessible but useful. Here are some suggestions.

Goal ideas
Make a friend you can ask work questions Learn about company culture Learn about new startups Discover an interesting new tool Get technical advice on a problem Find a practice interview partner Find an accountability buddy Trade resume reviews Meet a co-founder Get on a podcast Find an open source project Learn about other events
