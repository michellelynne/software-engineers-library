Making Opportunities Worksheet

Deciding which role you want next, and what it takes to get there

When you are interviewing for a new job, the interviewer will often ask why you want to work there. They want to know that you are interested in the product and have done at least some research on the company. They don’t want a canned answer. Obviously, all jobs have their boring parts, and no role is going to be all fun and games (except the role Director of Fun and Games, which is a real job for the Philadelphia Phillies).5

When you want a promotion, you need to be able to answer the same question. I know your heart (or your wallet) says “money please!” but your head needs to come up with a professional answer. You should also know for yourself, to help you establish your goals. So take a minute and write it down for me. Think about which role you want next and why. Be specific; tell me what you expect that role to look like. Think about the day-to-day as well as the long-term responsibilities. If you had that dream job, think about what you want to have accomplished in three, six, or twelve months.
