Growth Mode

Desire, sacrifice, opportunity

Growth mode needs all three parts:

  • Desire to make a change
  • Sacrifice that creates the time for it
  • Opportunity to act

Bring your top priorities from the Goal Types worksheets, then we'll figure out if you have the desire, sacrifice and opportunity to act.

04

The Call

Growth mode is not a permanent state, it is a decision you make when all three parts are present. If one is missing, maintenance mode is the right answer for now.

Turning a growth type into a specific, actionable goal is the hardest step. Use AI to pressure-test the one you wrote.

AI Prompt
You are a career coach for software engineers. I want to grow in [growth type], specifically [top priority]. I am a [level and role] at a [company size and industry], and I have [minutes] minutes a week for it. Turn that into three candidate goals with different time horizons, each written as something I could tell whether I had achieved. For each, list the first three actions and how many weeks they take at my available time. Then tell me which of the three is the wrong goal for what I said I want, and why.
