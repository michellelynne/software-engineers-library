Senior Engineer Skills
Self-Assessment

Like any good teacher, the first day starts with a pop quiz. This worksheet helps you figure out which Senior Engineer skills you need to improve. Be as honest as possible, since you're the only one who will see it. The skills are organized by chapter so you can find more on each one in the book.

Use the rubric below to pick your level. The notes field is for projects and activities that are evidence of a skill, since one project can span many skills. At the end of the book you'll repeat this assessment to map your next step: more training, a talk with your manager, or a job hunt. Remember, every new job should leave room for growth, so you don't need to be perfect at every skill before you apply.

How to pick your level (Rubric, Table 1.3)
JJunior

No real knowledge yet. You've only heard of it, seen others do it, or tried a tutorial or school project. Not used in a professional setting.

MMid

You've done this under the guidance of a more senior engineer. Ready to try it independently, but not yet ready to teach it to someone else.

SSenior

Done it multiple times independently and ready to coach others. You could list it on your resume and speak confidently about it in an interview.
