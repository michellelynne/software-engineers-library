Senior Engineer Skills
Self-Assessment

Before you learn anything new, you should assess where you are. Self-awareness is a skill that you need to be a Senior Engineer. This worksheet helps you figure out which Senior Engineer skills you need to improve. Be honest, this is only for you.

Mark your level for each skill, J (Junior), M (Mid), or S (Senior), using the rubric below. Use the notes column for projects or activities that are evidence of a skill. These will be helpful reminders when you need to write about your accomplishments.

How to pick your level
JJunior

No real knowledge yet. You've only heard of it, seen others do it, or tried a tutorial or school project. Not used in a professional setting.

MMid

You've done this under the guidance of a more senior engineer. Ready to try it independently, but not yet ready to teach it to someone else.

SSenior

Done it multiple times independently and ready to coach others. You could list it on your resume and speak confidently about it in an interview.
