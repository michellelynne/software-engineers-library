Company Filters

Salary is the benefit we think about the most, but there are many options. Give each benefit an acceptable range so that you can compare full packages. The premise is that if a job hits lower in the range in one benefit, they should hit higher in the range for another area. If this is not a benefit that interests you or is relevant to where you live (like retirement benefits), you can leave it blank and not use it as a filter.

Many companies use “Cost of Labor” to help them identify the minimal amount of a benefit to offer, so the region you want to work in matters. AI can help you quickly change the question to different regions and collate that data.

03

Culture Filters

These filters are the “how” of work instead of the “what” of industries. If any other cultural filter is important to you but not listed, add that in.

Where do I want to work?
Remote / Hybrid / On-Site
If not remote, where can the company be located? Would I move to work for this company?
Examples: Any city. Only American East Coast cities. Anywhere in Japan. Anywhere in the world. Only English-speaking countries. Only within 100 miles of x location.
How much travel do I want to do?
Examples: Zero, I am a caretaker who needs to be home. Once a quarter, for company meetings or educational opportunities. All the time, I love traveling and want it to be part of my job.
How much growth are you looking for?
Examples: Fast-paced, constantly changing. Stable, but steady growth. Slow growth, but strong job security.
How much does work/life balance matter right now?
Examples: Very much, I would like to work a steady 40 hours per week. Not at all, I am looking to put my all into the company. In the middle, I can be on call and do some weekends, but generally a 40-hour week.
Company Size
Examples: Startup, Small Business (<100), Medium Business (100 – 1500), Large Business (1500+)

Hard Dealbreakers

Lines you would never cross. Each one should start with “I would never…”. Unlike the ranges above, these are non-negotiable.

Examples: “I would never work for a political campaign.” · “I would never work fully on-site.” · “I would never work in a vice-focused industry.”
