Newsletter Worksheet

A template to help you organize data for your Newsletters

A team newsletter makes your work visible to the people who decide what happens next. A regular release schedule is common: long enough to have substantial work done, but frequent enough to maintain your visibility. I have used Q4 to discuss the entire past year and Q1 to feature bigger initiatives for the next year. You can use any time frame that makes sense to you. Use this template to gather data right after you finish it so you don't forget. These are two options for two different audiences. Memos are for management; demos are for users. These are references; research the templates your organization uses and model yours after them. Use your Success Statements and other concrete data to make your case.

03

Key Insights

Key Insights include major accomplishments and plans for the next time period, presented in one or two paragraphs. The more teams they have, the less time anyone will have to read a memo from each team. Ask yourself, when writing, if someone only reads the Key Insights, what would your team's brand be?

No acronyms your reader would have to look up.
04

Impact

Showing instead of telling the results of your work. What can people do now that they couldn't before? Use visuals, including charts, adoption curves, cost savings, and anything that shows impact to the organization at a glance.

Chart or figure goes here
One image that a reader could understand without the caption
Which numbers are you showing, where do they come from, and what should the reader conclude?
05

Highlights

The expanded details from the Key Insights summary. Show progress on long-running projects and anything completed. Include any teams you collaborated with. They will be reading this and deserve recognition.

Always include impact, similar to Success Statements.
06

Lowlights

Focusing just on the positives shows a lack of self-awareness. These can include incidents, changes in team composition, or missed deadlines. Keep these short, one sentence each, with a max of three.

What happened, and what changed because of it. Three at most.
07

Key Investments

The plan for the next time period. Your audience will expect to see progress on these, so only list what you have the resources for.

What you are working on next, and why it is worth the time.
