Branding Review

Everyone has a brand, both internally at their organization and externally in the community. Your brand is the impression people have of you or the feeling they get when your name is mentioned. A positive brand can open doors to new opportunities. A neutral or negative brand can lead to you being forgotten or worse. Use this worksheet to decide what you want your brand to be and how to implement it.

"It's this simple: You are a brand. You are in charge of your brand."
Tom Peters
