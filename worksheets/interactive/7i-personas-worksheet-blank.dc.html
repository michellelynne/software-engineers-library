Personas

Your project

When talking about MVP, I mentioned “a user”. But of course, in the case study and most applications, there are multiple user types, not one monolithic “user.” Even if everyone sees the same pages, you will at least need someone to handle operations or debugging. So let’s start to fill in the personas. You should gather this from market research and user interviews. If you can’t do those yet, create personas based on what you know about them.

Want to see it filled in? See the Renovation Revolution example.
◇ AI Prompt · Market research
"You are a market researcher helping me understand the users of [product in one or two sentences, and the market or region it serves]. Identify the distinct user types this product needs. For each one, give me their likely demographics, daily context, motivations, pain points, and behaviors, and mark each item as either supported by public evidence or an assumption I still need to test. Then write ten interview questions I could ask a real user of the type I most need to validate. Cite your sources where you have them."
Name each user type at the top of its card, then fill in the details. One item per line in the list fields. Add a card for every internal role too, such as the engineer on call and whoever answers support requests.
◇ AI Prompt
"You are a product researcher helping me pressure-test user personas. Here is my product in one paragraph: [paste]. Here are the personas I have written: [paste]. For each one, tell me which motivations and pain points are actually assumptions rather than research findings, name the questions I would have to ask a real user to confirm each assumption, and point out any user type I have missed."
