Proposal Memo

Renovation Revolution

This is the Renovation Revolution case study from the book, filled in so you can see what a finished brainstorm looks like before you write your own. Renovation Revolution is a marketplace app connecting homeowners with contractors for renovation projects.

Want a blank one for your own product? Use the blank Proposal Memo worksheet.
1

Audience & Goal

The audience is usually your manager, but they may share it upward or ask for stakeholder feedback. Use your Organization Context research to name a goal of theirs that this memo will address.

Who is this memo for?
Which of their goals does this memo address?
2

Blockers

List what is preventing that goal from being reached today.

Examples: the page takes eight seconds to load on mobile, so users drop off; new sign-ups need three support calls before they can start; the team ships slowly because every release is deployed by hand.

3

Ideas You Can Solve

List ideas for solving those blockers that are within your ability to solve.

Examples: add caching and lazy-load images to speed up the page; build a self-service onboarding flow; set up an automated deployment pipeline.

4

Top 3 Opportunities

Pick the three strongest ideas and weigh each one. The pros and cons make the trade-offs clear before you commit to a single proposal.

Example: "Automate the deployment pipeline." Pro: faster, safer releases; frees engineering time; aligns with the velocity goal. Con: upfront setup cost; the team needs to learn the new tooling.

5

ROI & Why It Has Waited

For those same three opportunities, weigh the return on investment, then name why it has not been done yet. A strong idea with good ROI usually has a reason it has waited, and naming it shows you understand the context.

ROI examples: new revenue, reduced churn, engineering time saved, against the cost of build and upkeep. Why-it-waited examples: it was overlooked, left out of the MVP, blocked by tech debt, or the resources were not there until now.

6

Choose the Strongest Idea

Pick one to propose. The strongest idea is a combination of high ROI and feasibility with your current technology and resources. 

The idea you will propose
Why you chose this one
7

Draft the Memo

This is the content to help you write the memo. Write it addressed to your audience, then move it into your organization's template.

What is the problem?
What is the solution?
What other solutions were researched but not chosen, and why?
