Prioritization for Engineers

When everything is a priority, nothing is a priority. These tools turn a pile of competing tasks into an ordered plan. As engineers, we aren't project management experts. However, we can use these simple tools to help prioritize for our team and ourselves. We'll be using two project management tools, the Impact / Effort Matrix and RICE scoring.

Time horizon How long do you have for this work? A quarter, half a year, a year?
01

List Your Projects

List every project, feature, or idea you or your team would like to complete in the time horizon. Give each one a short label of two or three words, then a fuller description. We'll be using the label for the rest of the steps.

02

Impact / Effort Matrix

The Impact/Effort Matrix is exactly what it sounds like. You are sorting the projects into four categories. Use your gut. You don't need to assign any numbers yet, just whether it is low or high.

1 Sort the labels into the matrix

Take the labels from your project list and write each into the square that fits: high or low impact, high or low effort.

03

RICE Scoring

Score each project so you can compare apples to apples. Reach = how many people it affects · Impact = 1 (low) to 4 (massive) · Confidence = how sure you are, as a % · Effort = engineer-sprints. The score is (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort.
