Organization Context

!
Treat this as a work document

Unlike the other worksheets in this book, this one is entirely about your current place of work, so it should be treated like a work document. Any information you gather from internal documentation is confidential. Keep your filled-in copy on your company drive, email, or wherever you store secure documents, so that casually filling in a worksheet does not lead to a catastrophic information leak.

This worksheet is designed to help you in meetings and when generating ideas. You will have the context you need to understand what leadership is talking about. At the same time, you can compare ideas to this and ask yourself, “Does this idea match the organization’s goals?”

1The Company
1.
Is your organization a non-profit, for-profit, or government entity?
2.
What products or services do they provide?
3.
If for-profit, how do they make revenue?
Examples: Subscription, selling a product, freemium.
4.
If a non-profit or government agency, what is their goal?
2Customers & Metrics
5.
Who are their customers?
6.
Is there anyone else using their products who is not a customer?
Example: A point-of-sale system is paid for by the store but is used by shoppers.
7.
What metrics do they use to judge company health?
Examples: Customer acquisition, churn rate, EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization).
8.
What were the goals for those metrics last quarter?
Example: For 2024 Q1, the goal was to add 1,000 customers.
9.
What were the results of those metrics last quarter?
Example: For 2024 Q1, we acquired 750 customers.
3Your Division
10.
What division are you in?
This is usually multiple teams with a single focus, run by a vice president or director.
11.
How does your division contribute to the company metrics?
12.
Does your division have any quarterly or yearly goals?
4Your Team
13.
What is the name of your team?
14.
How does your team contribute to the company metrics?
15.
How does your team contribute to the division goals?
16.
Does your team have any quarterly or yearly goals?
5You & Your Products
17.
Are you on any sub-team or squad within your team?
18.
What software product(s) do you contribute to, design, or maintain?
19.
How do those products fit the team, division, and company goals?
20.
Do you have any personal technical growth goals?
Example: I would like to learn more about data science next.
