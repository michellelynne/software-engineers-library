User Stories

Renovation Revolution

This is the Renovation Revolution case study from the book, filled in so you can see what finished user stories look like before you write your own. Renovation Revolution is a marketplace app connecting homeowners with contractors for renovation projects.

Want a blank one for your own product? Use the blank User Stories worksheet.
The format
As a [type of user], I want to [do something], so that [I get some benefit].

Example: As a returning customer, I want to save my payment details, so that I can check out faster next time.

1

Who Are Your Users?

List every type of person who will use the product. Include people who use it but are not the paying customer.

Examples: a first-time visitor, a returning customer, an account admin, a support agent who uses it on the customer's behalf.

2

Write the Stories

One story for each thing a user needs to do. Keep each one small enough to build and test on its own.

3

Happy Path Workflow

Pick your most important story and walk through the ideal path, step by step, as if everything goes right. This is the workflow you build first.

Example: opens the app, signs in, searches for what they need, reviews the options, makes a selection, confirms.
