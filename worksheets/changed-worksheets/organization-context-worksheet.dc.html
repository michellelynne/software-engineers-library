Organization Context

!
Treat this as a work document

Unlike the other worksheets in this book, this one is entirely about your current place of work, so it should be treated like a work document. Any information you gather from internal documentation is confidential. Rather than typing answers into this page, use Print / Save PDF to make a copy, and keep it on your company drive, email, or wherever you store secure documents. That way, casually filling in a worksheet will not lead to a catastrophic information leak.

This worksheet should be filled out when you start a new job or team, or right now. It can even be helpful before an interview to show your interest in an organization. It can be used as a rubric when generating new ideas: does that idea match the goals of your organization?
