Roles and Resources

3

Permissions and Scopes

A permission pairs a role with an action on a resource, such as "homeowners can see projects". The scope then narrows it from every record of that resource in the database down to the ones that role should actually reach, such as "only the projects they own" or "read only, no edits". Using your roles and resources, write out each permission and its scope.
