Build vs Buy

Common features for a new application

Almost every new application needs the same foundational pieces. You don’t have unlimited time, so you can’t build all of them. For each common feature below, decide whether you’ll build it in-house or buy an existing tool or service — and note why. Add your own product-specific features at the bottom.

Everyone’s problem?
A universal need like auth or payments. The solution almost definitely exists — lean Buy.
Your industry’s?
A vendor probably exists. Research fit and customization before you build.
Your core competency?
Unique to your company and where your value lives — this is your farm. Build it.
