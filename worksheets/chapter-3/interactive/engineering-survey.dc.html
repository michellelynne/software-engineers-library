Engineering Survey

Popular Tools

When you are ready to use an external tool to solve your problem, remember that it is a popularity contest. Software that people already know how to use reduces training time and makes it easier to hire. If there is already a large pool of experts, the organization doesn’t have to spend money on training. The question is, how do you know what’s popular? Use this survey with your team and nearby teams to get you started.
