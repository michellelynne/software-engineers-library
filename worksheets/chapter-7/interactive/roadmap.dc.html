MVP Roadmap

“Putting in the work upfront to really make sure that you understand the requirements from the stakeholders… saves a lot of time later on.”

Let’s practice building a roadmap by using the MVP.

01

What is the hypothesis for the MVP?

02

Resources

We need to gather the team resources. Never assume someone can work 8 hours a day, unless you have cleared all their other work, meetings and personal commitments. If they work more than you expect on a project, that’s a bonus. The engineers can have a focus which limits the tickets they pick up, or they can handle anything. I have added in a small team as an example.

03

Tickets

Only what proves the hypothesis. For each ticket ask: is it critical to a customer posting a project, or a contractor seeing one? If not, it goes on the “maybe later” list. Skip onboarding and seed sample data instead.
